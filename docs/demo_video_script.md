# CardioLens 3D: demo video script (target 6 min)

The rules ask for 3–10 minutes on YouTube (unlisted is fine), showing the project running and explaining the approach, with English audio or subtitles.
Record at 1920×1080 in Chrome at 100% zoom. Use OBS or the Windows Game Bar (Win+Alt+R). Run the app with `docker compose up` so the header shows **Model: server**.

| Time | Screen | What to say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:25 | Title card, then the app with the default patient (62 M, typical angina). Let the heart beat and particles flow. | "Risk scores are just numbers. A cardiologist thinks in arteries. CardioLens takes 54 routine clinical values, predicts coronary artery disease and stenosis in each of the three major arteries, and paints those predictions onto a real 3D heart, with the reasons behind each one." |
| 0:25–1:05 | Rotate and zoom the heart. Click **LAD**, then **LCX**, then **RCA** and let the camera fly to each. Switch to **Thorax** and back. | "This is real anatomy from BodyParts3D, the dataset the brief recommends. Each artery is its own mesh, LAD with its diagonals, the circumflex, the RCA with its PDA, so the colour of every vessel comes straight from that vessel's model output. The muscle is tinted by the territory each artery supplies, the particles follow centrelines we extracted from the meshes, and the heart beats at this patient's pulse rate." |
| 1:05–1:40 | Point at the right rail: CAD headline, interval, verdict, vessel bars, bullseye. | "On the right: the calibrated probability of CAD with a bootstrap interval, and a conformal prediction set. At 90% confidence it tells you when the model cannot decide, rather than forcing a yes or no. The bullseye is the AHA 17-segment map cardiologists use, coloured by vessel territory." |
| 1:40–2:30 | Click **LAD** in "Why this estimate". Read the sentence aloud. Hover a few bars. Open **Measurements**. | "Every number is explained. These are exact additive contributions, not sampled approximations: they add up exactly to the patient's log-odds. Below, every physiological measurement sits next to its reference range and its share of the prediction." |
| 2:30–3:20 | **Live what-if**: drag Age down, toggle Typical chest pain off, raise EF. Watch the heart recolour in real time. Then click **Apply all** under Modifiable factors. | "Everything is live. Each edit re-scores the patient in a few milliseconds and the heart recolours as you go. Modifiable factors shows how the estimate moves if an out-of-range value is brought back into range. We label it as model sensitivity, not a treatment effect, and we flag the counter-intuitive LDL direction, which reflects treatment in the source population." |
| 3:20–3:50 | Load "44 F, atypical pain", then "70 M, prior-infarct pattern". Then **Similar patients**, and click a row. | "Low-risk patient: all teal. Prior-infarct pattern: Q waves, wall-motion abnormality, low EF, and the LAD lights up. Similar patients shows the twelve closest real cases and what angiography actually found, so the clinician sees evidence, not just a score." |
| 3:50–5:05 | **Model evidence** tab: metrics table, ROC, calibration, decision curve, algorithm comparison, sanity checks. | "How we validated it. LAD, LCX, RCA and Cath are never inputs, and a test checks that. The whole pipeline (algorithm choice, hyper-parameters, calibration, thresholds, conformal quantiles) is fitted inside each training fold of repeated nested cross-validation. We compared nine algorithms; simple, transparent models were as good as boosting on 303 patients. One idea made the biggest difference: vessel stenosis implies CAD, so we model P(vessel) as P(CAD) times P(vessel given CAD). That improved vessel AUC and makes the heart anatomically consistent: no red artery on a 'no CAD' heart, verified on every out-of-fold prediction. The sanity checks show a label-permutation AUC of 0.5, and how a leaked model would look." |
| 5:05–5:35 | Stop the server. Reload: the header shows **Model: in-browser**. Click a patient; the numbers are identical. | "The same exported model runs in the browser with identical arithmetic, tested to 1e-9 on all 303 patients. It needs no GPU and keeps working offline." |
| 5:35–5:55 | Show `config/registry.json` and `anatomy/anatomy_registry.json` briefly, then **Export report** with the print preview. | "Targets, meshes and features are all config-driven, so adding a vessel or a lab value doesn't need a redesign. And one click produces a printable summary." |
| 5:55–6:10 | Disclaimer banner close-up, then end card with the GitHub link. | "CardioLens is decision support and education, not a diagnosis. Thanks for watching." |

Tips: record the voice-over separately and lay it over the screen capture. Add English subtitles in YouTube Studio by pasting the "What to say" column.
